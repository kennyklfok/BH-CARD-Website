Device follow-up

Pacemaker Clinic

The Pacemaker Clinic monitors and manages implanted cardiac devices such as pacemakers and implantable cardioverter defibrillators (ICDs).

What to Expect

  • Your device will be checked using specialized equipment to make sure it is working properly.
  • Battery status, heart rhythm information, and device settings will be reviewed.
  • Settings may be adjusted if needed to optimize device performance.
  • You will have the opportunity to schedule your next follow-up appointment before leaving the clinic.
