Wearable heart monitor

Holter & Event Monitors

Holter monitors and event monitors are portable devices that record your heart rhythm while you go about your day.

A Holter monitor usually records for 24 hours. An event monitor may be worn for up to two weeks.

Holter Monitor

  • Five stickers called electrodes will be placed on your chest.
  • Do not get the device wet. No bathing, showering, or swimming.
  • Write down any symptoms in the diary you are given.

Event Monitor

  • Three stickers called electrodes will be placed on your chest.
  • Do not bathe or shower while wearing the monitor.
  • You will be shown how to change the stickers so the monitor can be removed for bathing or showering.
  • Write down any symptoms in the diary you are given.

Returning the Monitor
